My story

From South London to New York City, and back with a plan.

I'm a first-generation professional who was never handed a map. In year 12 I became an Amos Bursary scholar, and suddenly I was sitting on panels, meeting people in rooms I didn't know existed, and spending a month in New York City interning at NYU.

That changed what I thought was possible. Now I'm at Nottingham, building partnerships with top firms for the ACS, and sharing everything I learn so the next person doesn't have to figure it out alone.
